About Eventsphere

Connecting students with amazing events across India

Our Mission

To create a vibrant ecosystem where students can discover, participate in, and organize meaningful events that enhance their academic and personal growth.

At Eventsphere, we believe that events are more than just gatherings – they're opportunities for learning, networking, and personal development. Our platform connects students with events that matter to them, while giving educational institutions and organizations the tools they need to reach their target audience.

Our Story

Eventsphere was founded in 2023 by a group of college students who were frustrated with the fragmented nature of event discovery on campus. Important workshops, guest lectures, and networking opportunities were often missed simply because students didn't know they were happening.

What started as a simple bulletin board has grown into India's leading platform for student events, connecting thousands of students with hundreds of educational institutions and organizations across the country.

Our Values

Innovation

We constantly seek new ways to improve the event discovery and management experience for both students and organizers.

Community

We believe in the power of bringing people together and fostering meaningful connections through events.

Trust

We are committed to creating a safe and reliable platform where students can discover verified events from trusted organizers.
